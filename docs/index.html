Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a station not playing or stuck connecting?

Radio broadcasters occasionally update their streaming servers. Pull down on the Stations screen to refresh the catalog right away—iTarab Radio also syncs updated streams automatically once every 24 hours. If a station remains offline, please report it via the button above so we can update or add a backup stream.

How can I request a new Arabic radio station?

Click Report a Broken Station / Request a Station above and include the station name, country (e.g., Yemen, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Egypt, Lebanon, Morocco), and its official website or stream URL if you know it.

Does iTarab Radio keep playing in the background?

Yes. Audio continues playing when you switch apps or lock your iPhone or iPad, and you can pause, resume, or skip stations directly from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

How do I set a Sleep Timer?

Tap the mini player bar at the bottom of the screen to open the full player, then tap the moon or timer icon to stop playback automatically after 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.

Where are my Favorites saved?

Your favorite stations and recently played shortcuts are stored locally on your device. No account is required, and no personal data is sent to our servers.
