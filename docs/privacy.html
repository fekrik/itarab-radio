Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026
Data Not Collected: iTarab Radio does not require an account, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

iTarab Radio is designed so you can listen to live Arabic radio stations privately. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

To provide a fast and personalized listening experience, iTarab Radio stores the following preferences solely on your device:

  • Your saved Favorites station list
  • Your Recently Played stations
  • The cached public radio station catalog for offline browsing

This data never leaves your device and is automatically removed if you uninstall the app.

3. Live Audio Streams & Catalog Updates

When you play a radio station or when the app checks once every 24 hours for updated station links from our public GitHub catalog, your device connects directly over HTTPS to the radio broadcaster's streaming server or GitHub's content delivery network to receive the audio stream or public JSON catalog. Like any standard internet connection, those external servers receive your device's IP address solely to deliver the requested stream or file. Individual radio broadcasters are responsible for their own server logs and policies.

4. Microphone & Sensitive Permissions

iTarab Radio does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or precise location. Audio playback uses standard media playback capabilities only.

5. Children's Privacy

Because iTarab Radio collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes & Contact

If this privacy policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or app support, please visit the iTarab Radio Support Page or open an inquiry at github.com/fekrik/itarab-radio/issues.
